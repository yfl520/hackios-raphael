HakiOS is an independent experimental XNU port and is not affiliated with
Apple, Qualcomm or Xiaomi. Product names identify compatible inputs or hardware.

Apple system files, SDKs, fonts, device firmware, private calibration, third-party
apps or a flashable system. Obtain external inputs under their own terms.
